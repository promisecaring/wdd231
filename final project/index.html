About Our Club

Drift Racing Club is a community of motorsport enthusiasts dedicated to promoting competitive drifting through professional events, driver development, racing education, and safe driving practices.

Whether you are an experienced driver, an aspiring racer, or simply a fan of drifting, our club provides opportunities to participate, learn, and enjoy the adrenaline-filled world of drift racing.
