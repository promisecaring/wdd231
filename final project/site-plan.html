Drift Racing Club

WDD 231 Final Project Site Plan

Site Name

Drift Racing Club

The name "Drift Racing Club" was selected because it represents a community dedicated to the exciting motorsport of drifting. The website will serve as an information hub for drift racing enthusiasts by showcasing events, professional drivers, drift cars, and membership opportunities.

Site Purpose

The purpose of the Drift Racing Club website is to promote drift racing by providing information about the club, upcoming events, featured drivers, drift cars, membership opportunities, safety practices, and club news.

Target Audience

  • Drift racing enthusiasts
  • Motorsport fans
  • Young adults interested in racing
  • Professional and amateur drivers
  • People interested in joining the club
  • Event spectators and sponsors

User Scenarios

  1. When is the next drift event?
  2. How do I register for an event?
  3. Who are the featured drivers?
  4. What safety equipment is required?
  5. How can I become a club member?
